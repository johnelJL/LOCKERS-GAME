What is the Lockers Game?

Imagine a hallway with a row of lockers, all closed. One hundred students walk by the lockers one after another. The first student opens every locker. The second student toggles every second locker, the third toggles every third locker, and so on. Which lockers remain open after every student has taken a turn?

The surprising answer is that only lockers in perfect square positions stay open. Each perfect square has an odd number of factors, which means it is toggled an odd number of times. All other lockers are toggled an even number of times and end up closed.

Use the simulator to adjust the number of lockers and students, then explore the pattern. Highlight perfect squares and filter the view to better see the structure.
